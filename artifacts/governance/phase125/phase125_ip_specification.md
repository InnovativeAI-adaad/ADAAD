## Abstract

A method and system for receiving, validating, deliberating, and ratifying proposed changes to
a constitutional invariant registry governing an autonomous AI mutation promotion system. The
system structurally enforces quorum requirements and sole-authority ratification constraints
through a combination of machine-validated structural gates, CI/CD pipeline enforcement, and
cryptographic signing requirements. No automated path to constitutional change exists or can
be added without violating structural invariants enforced by the system's Constitutional
Evolution Loop (CEL).

## Novel Claims

### Claim 1 — Structural Ratification Inviolability
A governance system wherein the ratification authority for constitutional amendments is
architecturally fixed to a single keyholder (HUMAN-0) and cannot be delegated, simulated, or
bypassed by any code path, workflow, or multi-party arrangement within the system.

### Claim 2 — Machine-Validated Proposal Structure Gate
A CI validation pipeline that structurally enforces minimum proposal quality requirements
(identifier format, rationale word count, quorum confirmation) as a precondition for entry into
the governance deliberation queue, with automatic rejection and labelling of non-conforming proposals.

### Claim 3 — FGCON Quorum Invariant Integration
Integration of the Federation Governance Consensus quorum constraint (Phase 119, FGCON-QUORUM-0)
into a community-facing amendment proposal workflow, structurally preventing a single contributor
from both proposing and ratifying the same constitutional amendment.

### Claim 4 — Append-Only Constitutional Provenance
Each ratified amendment is appended to the constitutional registry with full provenance metadata
including proposer identity, deliberation timeline, FGCON quorum proof, and HUMAN-0 GPG signature
reference, producing an immutable audit trail of constitutional evolution.

## Prior Art Differentiation

Existing open-source governance systems (e.g. Gitcoin Governor, Compound Governor Bravo) implement
token-weighted voting mechanisms that permit automated ratification when quorum thresholds are met.
The present system differs in that:

1. No token-weighting, stake, or vote-count mechanism can substitute for HUMAN-0 ratification.
2. The quorum check is a structural prerequisite, not a ratification mechanism.
3. The constitutional registry is governed by the same CEL that governs all system mutations,
   making constitutional amendments subject to the same cryptographic proof requirements as code changes.
