## Notes for counsel and reviewers

- Ordering is deterministic by phase number, source date, and artifact path.
- `PRIOR_ART_REGISTRY.json` is the machine-readable source for this index.
- `source_artifact_sha256` values are deterministic file-content hashes computed from checked-in source artifacts; they are not generated timestamps.
- Where a source artifact contains a placeholder digest, this index preserves it verbatim rather than substituting a legal attestation.
